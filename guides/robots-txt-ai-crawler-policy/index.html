Robots.txt AI Crawler Policy Audit

Many teams sell AI search visibility work before checking the simplest technical question: can the relevant crawler reach the pages that explain the offer?

Common Policy States

  • Explicit allow: The site intentionally permits a crawler.
  • Explicit block: The site intentionally blocks a crawler.
  • Implicit/default: The crawler is not named; behavior depends on broader robots.txt rules.
  • Conflicting: A specific allow/block conflicts with a broad rule.

Example Review Block

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

This example allows OpenAI search discovery while blocking model training crawler access. A real audit should also inspect sitemap coverage, page crawlability, schema, and internal links.

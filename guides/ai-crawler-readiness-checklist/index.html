AI Crawler Readiness Checklist

This checklist is for SEO teams, GEO agencies, and SaaS marketers who need a technical preflight before broader AI search work. It does not predict or guarantee AI citations.

Use it to find crawler policy gaps, indexability issues, and missing machine-readable context before selling a larger GEO or AEO engagement.

1. Robots.txt AI Crawler Policy

  • Review explicit rules for OAI-SearchBot, GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Google-Extended.
  • Check whether important commercial pages are accidentally blocked by broad disallow rules.
  • Document whether the policy is intentional, absent, or conflicting.

2. Sitemap And Crawl Path

  • Confirm robots.txt references at least one sitemap.
  • Check whether pricing, product, docs, case study, and comparison pages appear in XML sitemaps.
  • Look for orphaned or JavaScript-only pages that need internal links.

3. Schema And Entity Clarity

  • Validate Organization, Product, SoftwareApplication, FAQ, Article, and Breadcrumb schema where relevant.
  • Make sure brand, product category, audience, and offer are clear in visible copy.
  • Do not add fake claims or unsupported review markup.

4. llms.txt Reality Check

llms.txt can be useful as a site map for humans and AI tooling, but it is not a ranking guarantee. Treat it as optional documentation, not a replacement for crawlable pages and schema.

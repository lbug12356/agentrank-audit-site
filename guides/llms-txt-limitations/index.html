llms.txt Limitations

llms.txt can be a useful content map for AI tools, but it should not be sold as a guaranteed ranking lever. AgentRank Audit treats it as one optional signal inside a broader technical readiness check.

Higher-Confidence Signals

  • Clear robots.txt crawler policy.
  • Accessible sitemap.xml.
  • Structured data on important pages.
  • Crawlable pricing, docs, customer proof, and FAQ pages.
  • No unexpected 401, 403, or 429 blocks for legitimate crawler-style requests.

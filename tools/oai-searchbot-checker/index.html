OAI-SearchBot Robots.txt Checker

OAI-SearchBot is OpenAI's crawler for search result discovery. This page helps site owners and SEO teams audit whether their robots.txt stance is explicit and intentional.

What To Check

  • Whether User-agent: OAI-SearchBot exists.
  • Whether it is allowed, blocked, or only partially allowed.
  • Whether robots.txt links to a sitemap.
  • Whether important pricing, docs, and customer proof pages are crawlable.
